Privacy Policy

Self Budget by DevCalApps

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

1. Zero External Data Collection & Tracking

Self Budget is engineered as a privacy-first, offline-first application. We believe your personal financial records belong exclusively to you.

  • No Server Infrastructure: We do not operate external databases, tracking servers, or remote telemetry systems.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: No advertising IDs, third-party SDKs, tracking cookies, or diagnostic collection tools are embedded in the app.
  • No Data Monetization: We never collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal or financial data to any third party.

2. On-Device Local Storage

All financial entries created within Self Budget—including transactions, income, expense categories, monthly budgets, savings goals, custom accounts, and exchange rates—are stored locally on your Android device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Room Persistence Library).

Your data remains entirely on your physical device unless you explicitly choose to backup or export it.

3. Google Drive Cloud Sync

Self Budget offers an optional, automated Google Drive Cloud Sync feature:

  • Private Sandbox Storage (appDataFolder): Cloud backups are saved to a hidden, private folder in your personal Google Drive using the official https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata OAuth scope.
  • Isolated File Access: Backup files (self_budget_cloud_backup.json) are stored strictly within your personal Google Account storage quota. Other Google Drive applications cannot view or access this data.
  • Zero Developer Access: DevCalApps has zero access to your Google Account credentials, Google Drive storage, or backup data.

4. Manual Export & Backup Options

You retain full data portability and can export your records at any time:

  • JSON Backup Export: Creates a structured, cent-safe JSON backup file handed to the Android Share Sheet for local saving or personal storage.
  • CSV Transaction Export: Exports your transaction history as a standard CSV spreadsheet file for use in spreadsheet software.

5. Security & Biometric Protection

  • Biometric Authentication: If enabled, device-level biometric authentication (Fingerprint, Face Unlock) uses Android’s native BiometricPrompt system framework.
  • Hardware Security: Biometric templates remain securely isolated inside your device’s hardware Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) and are never accessed by or transmitted to the app.

6. User Control & Data Erasure

You maintain 100% control over your data at all times:

  • Data Reset: Erase all local accounts, transactions, and settings at any time using Clean Sweep / Reset Data in the app Settings.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: Manage or remove cloud backups directly inside your Google Account settings (Google Drive Web → Settings → Manage Apps → Self Budget → Delete hidden app data).
