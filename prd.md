# Product Requirement Document (PRD): Self Budget App

## 1. Product Vision & Goals
**Self Budget** is an intuitive, offline-first personal finance application for Android. It enables users to take full control of their personal finances through fast expense logging, multi-account and wallet balance tracking, smart credit card / loan / mortgage debt payoff balance reduction, privacy balance toggles, intelligent category budgeting with daily pace safeguards, graphical analytics, multi-currency support, speech-to-text voice logging with big round mic buttons across all forms, camera receipt attachments, ML Kit OCR scanning, customizable theme modes, bi-weekly & monthly paycheck scheduling, biometric security privacy shields, Android 13+ runtime notification permissions, unified full-screen modal UX across all forms, styled empty state cards across all tabs, persistent top app bar ("Self Budget" top-left & dynamic page title top-right), interactive `[ Monthly | Annual YTD ]` analytics timeframe toggle, automated 8:00 AM 1-day early bill due reminder WorkManager background worker with Option C Neobank & Tech notification emojis (`📆` `🎯` `🚀` `💳`), potential duplicate recurring entry posting confirmation safeguards, universal custom Material 3 delete confirmation safeguards, smart recurring pattern auto-detection & suggestion banners, automatic recurring bill to budget page synchronization & committed expense badges, instant success feedback Toast & debounced button states on recurring posting, direct card tap-to-edit interactions with pencil and trash icon removal, high-contrast Dark Mode & Light Mode Neobank elevated card contrast calibration, full-page custom category & payment account creation screens with persistent top app bar Save buttons, 1-tap dashboard account card edit & delete flow with M3 trash badge confirmation dialogs, clean minimalist 60.dp `+` icon button card in the Accounts Carousel, full-screen overlay `CategorySelectionModal` & `AccountSelectionModal` with live search bar & smooth native `LazyColumn` touch scrolling, automatic first-letter capitalization enforcement across all text entry boxes, About Developer section with direct contact email support (`dev.cal.apps@gmail.com`), 1-tap in-app Clean Sweep / Reset All Data tool in Settings, clean $0.00 initial cash flow state when no category budgets are set, concise greyed-out helper typography across all forms, tap-to-open recurring details modal with in-card Delete, Archive, and Mark Done actions, friendly "Over Budget" cash flow headline terminology, 4-column Monthly Cash Flow layout (`Income` | `Budgets` | `Unbudgeted` | `Paid`), dedicated full-screen "View All Accounts & Wallets" page with search bar & type filters, persistent cycle-aware recurring bill posted status badges (`Posted for Aug ✅` & persistent greyed-out `Posted` state with single checkmark), reimagined modern Neobank category limit cards with category avatar boxes, pill badges, extra bold numbers, floating percentage pills, & bottom metrics insets, asset account strict filtering for Savings Goals (disallowing credit cards and debt accounts), stacked side-by-side modal button UX, 1-tap Savings Goal Card tap-to-edit with M3 trash badge delete confirmation safeguard, single floating green `+` FAB on Savings Goals page, unified dark surface styling across all hero cards in Dark Mode, single system default currency source of truth across all screens and forms, dedicated full-screen Net Worth History & Asset/Liability Breakdown modal, standardized 2-decimal-place currency formatting across all screens and input fields, strict Income exclusion safeguard in Cash Flow Payments calculation, streamlined home dashboard with top 5 recent activity preview, and safe session management via Google Authentication.

## 2. Codebase Organization: Feature-Based Architecture
The codebase strictly follows a **Feature Package Structure**:

```text
com.selfbudget.app/
├── core/
│   ├── auth/           # AuthManager & Google Credential Manager integration
│   ├── security/       # BiometricSecurityManager (Fingerprint, Face ID, PIN)
│   ├── ui/             # MonthYearHeader, AddCustomCategoryDialog, AddCustomAccountDialog, EditCustomAccountDialog, CategorySelectionModal, AccountSelectionModal, AccountsViewAllModal, NetWorthHistoryModal
│   ├── util/           # AccountBalanceCalculator (Smart Debt Balance Reduction), BillReminderWorker (8 AM 1-Day Early Reminders), CsvExporter, VoiceParser, ReceiptOcrScanner, NotificationHelper
│   └── theme/          # Material 3 Theme (High-Contrast WCAG AA Light & Dark Mode ColorSchemes)
├── data/
│   ├── local/          # Room AppDatabase (v12), DAOs (Transaction, Category, Budget, User, Recurring, Account)
│   ├── model/          # Entities (TransactionEntity, CategoryEntity, BudgetEntity, UserEntity, RecurringTransactionEntity, AccountEntity)
│   └── repository/     # ExpenseRepository
├── feature/
│   ├── auth/           # LoginScreen with Google Auth & AppLockScreen (Solid Privacy Shield)
│   ├── dashboard/      # HomeScreen (Persistent Top App Bar: "Self Budget" Top-Left & Active Page Title Top-Right), AddEntryPointScreen (single global "+" entry chooser: Income / Expense / Budget / Recurring), DashboardContent, Balance Banner, Accounts Carousel, Privacy Toggle, GoalsSection (EditGoalDialog)
│   ├── accounts/       # AccountsScreen (Dedicated Accounts & Net Worth Manager)
│   ├── transaction/    # AddIncomeDialog & AddExpenseDialog (Smart Target Debt Account Selector for Credit Card / Loan / Mortgage Payoffs) & EditTransactionDialog
│   ├── budget/         # BudgetScreen & SetBudgetDialog (Stacked Row 1: Cancel | Save, Row 2: Delete Category Budget with M3 Confirmation Modal)
│   ├── recurring/      # RecurringScreen & SetRecurringDialog (Persistent Cycle-Aware Posted Status: "Posted for Aug ✅" Badge & Greyed-Out "Posted" Button State)
│   ├── analytics/      # AnalyticsScreen
│   └── profile/        # SettingsScreen
├── ui/
│   ├── MainViewModel.kt
│   └── theme/
└── MainActivity.kt
```
